## Accuracy policy

- Conceptual renderings and generated diagram worlds do **not** depict built plans or measured physical outcomes.
- The four social-theory types are a **proposed normative/analytical comparison**; real social systems are hybrid and historically contingent.
- Classical philosophers are used to illustrate tension between competing theories, not to claim their endorsement of Zistshahr.
- Separate verified article text from inferred design ideas. Flag inaccessible documents as **heading-only** until independently read.
