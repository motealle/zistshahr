# A Proposed Typology of Social Theories

Transcribed and lightly normalized for display from `original/4050815- نظریه اجتماعی به انگلیسی-J.xlsx`, primarily `Sheet1` rows 1–6. This matrix is the Zistshahr project's **proposed** comparison of motivational, institutional, and leadership foundations. Do not treat it as a neutral, exhaustive classification of all historical societies.

| Comparative criterion | Society of Domination | Market Society | Prophetic Model of Society (Ummah) | Anarchist Society |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary Motivational Force | Self-Interest-Based Motivation | Greed-Based Self-Regarding Motivation | Affection-Based Other-Regarding Motivation | Common-Interest-Based Cooperative Motivation |
| Institutional Mechanism for Social Order | Fear of Ruling Power | Free Competition | Human Dignity | Cooperation |
| Basis for Allocating Social Positions and Responsibilities | Capacity to Generate Power | Capacity to Generate Wealth | Superior Capacity for Self-Sacrifice and Consciousness-Raising | Small-Scale Assemblies and Councils |

The alternate sheet `Sheet1 (2)` has minor phrasing differences, such as “Capacity to Accumulate and Exercise Power” and “Capacity to Generate and Accumulate Wealth”; preserve the original workbook for source fidelity. The immersive V02 atlas presents columns in the visual sequence Domination → Market → Anarchist Society → Prophetic Ummah to make the four lenses flow naturally; this is a *presentation order*, not a change in theory or the workbook source order.

The English exhibition should contextualize the relationship to urban form: power/oversight, competition/economic concentration, cooperation/local assemblies, dignity/care/neighborhood institutions. These are design questions and normative intentions, not measured causal outcomes.
