THE WORK BEHIND THE VISION

Not just a city.
A different idea of living.

Zistshahr is a research-led proposal connecting urban form, social relations, small-scale production, ecology and the systems that sustain everyday life. This visual journey illustrates concepts; it is not a claim that a physical city has already been built.

THE FIRST QUESTION IS NEVER HOW TO BUILD.

Before the form,
the human vision.

Zistshahr asks a foundational question: which social relations should a city make possible? The comparison below is a proposed interpretive framework developed in the project's research materials—not a universal or exhaustive classification of societies.

01 / HOBBES · 1651

“Covenants, without the Sword, are but Words.”

Leviathan, XVII. A reference point for a theory of order mediated by sovereign power, not a complete description of Hobbes.
02 / ADAM SMITH · 1776

“Led by an invisible hand to promote an end...”

The Wealth of Nations, IV.ii. A point of departure for thinking about decentralized exchange and unintended social outcomes.
03 / IMMANUEL KANT · 1784

“The unsocial sociality of man.”

Idea for a Universal History, Proposition IV. The tension between sociability and antagonism is a problem for social design.
